# ZagZig Visibility Boost

Act as an expert direct-response copywriter, conversion rate optimization (CRO) specialist, and high-converting ClickFunnels UI/UX designer. Write human, conversational copy and provide precise ClickFunnels page layout instructions for a local business Lead Magnet Opt-In Funnel and Thank You Page built for ZagZig Marketing.

Brand & Strategic Context:

Brand Name: ZagZig Marketing

Brand Aesthetic: Bold, direct, minimalist, high-contrast black and white theme featuring distressed/textured sans-serif typography.

Platform: ClickFunnels (Classic or 2.0).

Offer: Free Custom Local SEO Audit (manually conducted and delivered within 24 hours).

Target Audience: Any local business owner wanting higher visibility and top-3 rankings on Google.

Core Media: A short, trust-building Video Sales Letter (VSL) embedded above the form.

Tone & Style: Conversational, direct, human, and no-fluff.

Formatting Constraint: Do NOT use em-dashes (—) anywhere in the copy across all sections.

Selected Core Page Messaging (Locked In):

Main Headline: "Are you invisible on Google?"

Subheadline / Solution Hook: "Watch this 2-minute video to see exactly why you aren't ranking in the Top 3 and how to fix it for free."

Form Micro-Copy / Section Header: "Tell us where to send your breakdown (takes 30 seconds):"

Form Inputs & Placeholders:

Name Field: First & Last Name

Email Field: Where should we email your video audit?

Business Name Field: Your Business Name

Website URL Field: yourwebsite.com (so we know where to look)

CTA Button Copy: "Show Me Why I’m Not Ranking"

Deliverables Required:

1. Full Opt-In Landing Page Copy & ClickFunnels UI Layout

Assemble the full landing page using ClickFunnels structural hierarchy (Sections, Rows, Columns, Elements):

Header / Hero Section: Logo placement, main headline, subheadline, and centered VSL video element layout.

Form Section: Row/Column configuration for the 4 input fields (Name, Email, Business Name, Website URL), action button, and friction-reducing micro-copy (e.g., "100% Free. No credit card required. No sales pitch.").

2. Thank You / Confirmation Page Copy & ClickFunnels UI Layout

Confirmation Header: Warm, conversational confirmation that their request was successfully received.

Expectation Setting Copy: Friendly explanation that a team member at ZagZig Marketing is manually reviewing their site and Google profile, and their custom video audit will land in their inbox within 24 hours.

Deliverability Alert Box: A stylized ClickFunnels warning element/alert box reminding them to check their Spam and Promotions folders.

3. ClickFunnels Styling & UI Design Instructions

Provide step-by-step ClickFunnels element settings to achieve the ZagZig Marketing aesthetic:

Color palette recommendations (HEX codes for dark/light contrast elements, background sections, and high-visibility CTA buttons).

Button design properties (hover effects, shadows, padding, typography styling).

Mobile responsiveness rules for form stacking and typography scaling inside the ClickFunnels editor.
